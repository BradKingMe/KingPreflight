# Product design

## Surface

KingPreflight is a local utility for reviewing a folder before sharing it. The screen is an operate surface: help a person choose a folder, understand likely risk signals, and inspect coverage in one sitting.

## Visual direction

Use the disciplined language of a field inspection sheet: calm paper-like backgrounds, ink-dark text, a deep spruce action color, and muted amber or brick only for review states. Keep lines fine and surfaces few. The metaphor explains the inspection task; avoid literal aircraft controls, gauges, stamps, or assurances of safety.

## Layout

- Desktop: narrow navigation rail, task heading, folder action, findings ledger, and a smaller coverage summary.
- Mobile: compact brand row, full-width folder action, stacked findings and summary, no horizontal table.
- Findings use readable text rows with priority, filename, and plain-language reason. Expand details inline.
- The sample view is explicitly labeled as fictional. Real scans show the selected folder name and uninspected coverage.

## Interaction and language

- The primary action is “Choose a folder.”
- Finding rows expand to show context; they do not mark a file safe.
- “Export report” downloads a local JSON report after a sample or scan exists.
- Use “need a look,” “review,” and “not inspected”; never say “safe” or “clean” as a verdict.
- Preserve keyboard focus, reduced-motion preferences, semantic controls, and mobile legibility.
